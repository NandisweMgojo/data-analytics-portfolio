The prepared dataset was imported into Power BI, where I explored patterns and relationships between different variables.

### 4. Dashboard Development

I created a multi-page Power BI dashboard containing charts and visualisations to communicate the results.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Mental Health Survey

The first dashboard page provides an overview of the survey responses, including depression status, stress levels by age group and depression status by age group.

### 2. Mental Health and Support

The second page explores contributing factors, suicidal thoughts by age group, mental health resource availability and perceived emotional support.

### 3. Support and Help-Seeking

The third page examines sources of support, barriers to seeking help and the demand for additional support opportunities.

## Key Findings

- Financial stress was the most frequently reported contributing factor.
- Fear of being judged was the most frequently reported barrier to seeking help.
- Friends were the most commonly reported source of support.
- Most respondents indicated that they would like more support opportunities.
- The 18–25 age group represented the largest group of survey responses.
